Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Who this covers

Grendel is a personal, self-hosted app operated by BrandonVB766 for their own use. Its only user is its owner. It isn't offered to anyone else.

Data Grendel accesses

With the owner's consent, Grendel reads the owner's own data from their Google Account through the Google Health API. Depending on the permissions the owner grants, this can include:

  • activity and fitness data, such as steps, distance, calories and workouts
  • health metrics, such as heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen and respiratory rate
  • sleep data, such as duration and sleep stages
  • body measurements and settings stored in the Google Health account

How the data is used

  • To show the owner their own data in private dashboards.
  • To calculate trends and statistics for the owner.
  • To let the owner's personal assistant, which runs on the same home server, answer the owner's questions about their own health. To answer a question, the relevant data may be sent to the AI model provider the assistant uses (currently Anthropic's Claude API), only to produce that answer.

The data is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone else, and never used to train AI models.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored on the owner's own server at home. That server can only be reached from the owner's home network or over the owner's VPN. Grendel doesn't store the data with any cloud service.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. The owner can stop Grendel's access at any time in their Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Grendel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
