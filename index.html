Grendel

A personal, self-hosted health dashboard for one person: its owner.

What it is

Grendel is a private app that BrandonVB766 built for their own use. It isn't a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it. It runs on the owner's own server at home and can only be reached from the owner's home network or over the owner's VPN.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's own health and fitness data from their Google Account through the Google Health API, such as activity, heart rate, sleep and other measurements from their fitness tracker.
  • Stores that data on the owner's home server.
  • Shows it in private dashboards and analyses it for the owner.
  • Lets the owner's personal assistant, which runs on the same server, answer the owner's questions about their own health data.
